# Decap: voice and copy rules

Written for: anyone writing website copy for decap.be (people or AI).

## Who we sound like

A fourth-generation workshop that builds things properly and can explain them simply. Confident
because of what we have done, never because of adjectives. Think of a master craftsman showing a
visitor around the workshop: precise, warm, a little proud, no sales pitch.

## Rules

1. **Every sentence carries a fact, a spec, a consequence for the reader, or something only Decap
   can say.** If a sentence would fit on any other company's website, delete it.
2. **No filler words:** symphony, harmonious, seamless(ly), unleash, elevate, journey, marvel,
   testament, world-class, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, revolutionary, unparalleled,
   "where X meets Y", "more than just", "take it to the next level", "crafting visions".
3. **Short sentences. Active voice. Concrete nouns.** "We mill every pipe on our own CNC" beats
   "Pipes are manufactured using advanced technology".
4. **Lead with what the reader gets**, then how. Specs support claims; they don't replace them.
5. **British English** spelling (colour, catalogue, centre), metric units, € with a space before
   the amount (€ 150), "excl. VAT".
6. **Names exactly as in FACTS.md:** Decap, Decap Herentals, SmartValve, Brass Core, Orchestrionix,
   Virtual Orchestrion, MIDIMapper, Atelier Frank Decap. Never IPU, uPhonic, VirtuPhonic,
   RhythmiCoil.
7. **Never invent** customers, numbers, prices, dates or quotes. Use `[TO CHECK: …]`.
8. **One clear next step per page**, phrased as what happens: "Request a consultation", "Send us
   photos of your organ", "Download the free trial". Never "Let's talk" or "Contact us" alone.
9. Headlines: short, plain, specific. Statements, not questions. No exclamation marks.
10. SEO: each page has one H1 (the hero title) containing its main search term in natural words
    ("dance organ", "organ restoration", "self-playing accordion", "MIDI conversion for book
    organs"); meta titles ≤ 60 characters, format "Topic | Decap Herentals"; meta descriptions
    140–160 characters, specific, with the benefit. Use the terms people search for (dance organ,
    fair organ, street organ, orchestrion, self-playing accordion, MIDI organ, organ pipes, book
    organ) in headings and body where natural.

## Examples

- ❌ "Step into a world where engineering marries artistry."
  ✅ "We have built organs in Herentals since 1933. Today we also make the self-playing instruments
  and the electronics that drive them."
- ❌ "Crafting visions? Let's talk."
  ✅ "Tell us where the organ will play. We reply with questions and a first idea."
