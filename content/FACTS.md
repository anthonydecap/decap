## Organs (core business)

- New organs: dance organs, fair organs, street organs, church/concert organs; for dance halls,
  cafés, fairgrounds, collectors, museums, churches.
- Two routes: **modular** (customer picks from proven modules) or **fully bespoke**.
- Standard scales: **105 and 121 keys**, but anything is possible.
- **Keys are no limit:** the Virtual Orchestrion player adds missing ranks virtually with
  in-house recorded samples and VSTs, so a 105-key organ also plays 121-key music.
- Sound: pipes, digital, or pipes and digital combined.
- Front instruments heritage: accordions (since 1933), drum kits, percussion, figures.
- Process: consultation → design and 3D render → production → voicing → delivery.
- Renders exist for some modular and some bespoke organs (images to be added).
- Restoration and MIDI installation: **Atelier Frank Decap**.

### MIDI conversion units (Decap product)
- Double-valve MIDI unit, 16 double-switching valves per unit, placed between key frame and main
  chest: book/roll operation stays completely intact; book and MIDI can play at the same time.
- Minimal dimensions, mounts in any orientation (even upside down).
- Any of the 16 MIDI channels; each valve assignable to any MIDI note with the included software
  (keeps tubing short and simple).
- Note length adjustable from −64 to +64 ms; note delay up to 500 ms (e.g. to sync with piano
  systems that need 500 ms pre-delay).
- Customers buy the units from Decap; installation by Atelier Frank Decap (or their own builder).

### Organ pipes (Decap product)
- Wooden pipes: bourdons, flutes, violins, reed pipes. Made on 5-axis CNC with Decap's own
  **PipeCreator** software (generates the G-code); in-house since 2016.
- Body in Ayous (Triplochiton scleroxylon): stable, pale yellow, virtually knot-free. Foot and mouth
  in hand-selected beech.
- Custom measurements with short delivery times; voicing by Decap's voicers or left to the customer.
- Range (MIDI note numbers):
  - Stopped: Bourdon 24–96 (6 octaves); Jazz Flute 60–96 (3 oct.); Vibraton 46–84 (3 oct.);
    Double Street Organ 60–96 (3 oct.)
  - Open: Flute (Flute 8′ etc.) 60–96 (3 oct.); Violins (alto, cello etc.) 48–96 (4 oct.)
  - Reed: Bariton 48–84 (3 oct.); Trumpet 60–96 (3 oct.)

## Instruments (self-playing, music included)

- **Accordion Pedestal & Cube**: since 2004; "one of the most loved and sold instruments in our
  catalogue"; real Italian accordion; PWM key action; FPGA control; closed-loop airflow; LFO
  vibrato/crescendo/diminuendo; MIDI in/out/thru; touchscreen MIDI/MP3 player with SD card;
  General MIDI sound module, speakers and subwoofer for accompaniment. Pedestal = freestanding;
  Cube = compact, transport-friendly.
- **Pipes (Pipe Cube / Pedestal)**: up to 7 ranks; built-in silent multistage blower; pressure FX

- **Percussion Unit**: drum kit plus tambourine, claves, woodblock, cowbell, shaker egg; full

## Orchestrionix: Brass Core (existing Prismic page `core`, to become `brass-core`)

- A voice-coil motor converts an audio signal into physical vibrations inside a pressure chamber,
  replicating a player's embouchure; the real brass instrument's resonance shapes the tone.
- Works with standard mouthpieces and tubing: trumpet, trombone and similar.
- Non-invasive: no drilling or modification of the instrument.
- Input: line-level audio (page lists 3.5 mm stereo line-in and mini XLR; external amplifier).
  12 V DC, 2 A; 120 × 90 × 70 mm. Accessories: trumpet clamp, trombone clamp.
- Users: theatres, sound designers, museums, studios, collectors. Testimonial on page: Circus Ronaldo.
- Stripe product price: €799 `[TO CHECK: current price]`.

## SmartValve (existing Prismic page `smartvalve`, keep)

- One valve per pipe with its own processor; airflow regulated more than 1,000 times per second;
  pressure 0–200% stored per pipe; 4 ms response; PID feedback; optical MIDI cable (100× MIDI speed,
  200 m+); LFO effects, vibrato, slope, decay. Proven in L'Explorateur (2025).
- Optical MIDI is an accessory of SmartValve.
